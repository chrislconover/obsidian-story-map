## Edit titles directly on the map

- Click any Activity, Task, or Story title to rename it inline.
- Long titles wrap automatically. The editor grows and shrinks with the text, and manual line breaks remain visible after saving.
- **Enter** saves, **Shift+Enter** inserts a line break, and **Esc** cancels. Clicking away also saves.
- Use the document button beside Activity and Task titles for full details; click the Story card outside its title for Story details.
- Renaming supports undo/redo without changing linked note filenames. Failed saves retain the draft for retry.
- Drag the header or card area outside the title to reorder items.

## 直接点击标题编辑

Activity、Task、Story 标题均可单击就地改名。长标题自动换行，输入框随内容增高或缩小，手动换行保存后仍完整显示。Enter 保存，Shift+Enter 换行，Esc 取消，点击其他位置也会保存。支持撤销、重做及保存失败后重试。
