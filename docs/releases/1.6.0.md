## Activity and Task drag-and-drop reordering

- Reorder Activities by dragging their headers. Their Tasks and Stories follow them.
- Reorder Tasks within an Activity or move them between Activities, keeping associated Stories together.
- Drop on **+ Activity** or an Activity’s **+ Task** space to move an item to the end.
- A blue insertion line indicates the drop position. Effective moves support undo/redo; unchanged drops do not add undo steps.
- Existing map files remain compatible. Requires Obsidian 1.8.10 or newer.

## Activity 与 Task 拖拽排序

现在可以拖拽 Activity 和 Task 标题调整顺序，Task 还可跨 Activity 移动，关联 Story 会一起移动。拖到 **+ Activity** 或对应 Activity 的 **+ Task** 区域可移至末尾。蓝色插入线提示放置位置，每次有效移动支持撤销与重做，原有地图文件保持兼容。
